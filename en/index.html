Why AI in French Real Estate & Construction?

A massive, under-digitised market

Real estate and construction account for ~20% of French GDP. Most players still rely on manual processes for document management, valuation, and client relations.

Immediate opportunity

Rising regulatory pressure

DPE, RE2020, Climate & Resilience Act — compliance mandates are forcing the sector to digitise. AI can automate compliance checks and reporting.

Catalyst

Long sales cycles = high ACV potential

Real estate cycles (6-18 months) generate massive volumes of untapped data. AI converts that data into competitive advantage — and large contracts.

Structural edge

Late adopters = first-mover window

Unlike fintech or e-commerce, French real estate has not yet had its AI revolution. The first entrant will verticalise and dominate.
